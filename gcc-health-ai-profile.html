About Us

What is GCC Health AI?

GCC Health AI is the leading professional community connecting healthcare providers, AI engineers, founders, and investors across the Gulf Cooperation Council and beyond.

  • A curated network of 300+ vetted professionals driving AI adoption in GCC healthcare
  • A platform for cross-border collaboration, partnerships, and deal flow
  • A knowledge hub accelerating innovation aligned with Vision 2030
  • Active in 6 markets: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman, India, Europe & USA
