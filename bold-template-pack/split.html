The New Approach

  • 1
    Ship smaller, ship faster Reduce batch sizes to accelerate feedback loops and reduce risk
  • 2
    Automate the boring parts CI/CD, testing, and monitoring free your team to focus on value
  • 3
    Measure outcomes, not output Lines of code and story points don't tell you if users are happy
  • 4
    Build psychological safety High-performing teams require an environment where failure is learning
